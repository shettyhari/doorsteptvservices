Common TV Problems

Facing One of These Issues?

These are the faults we fix most often. If yours isn't listed, just call — we've likely seen it before.

01

Sound but no picture

Usually a backlight strip or power-board fault. We test and replace only what's needed.

02

Picture but no sound

Speaker, audio IC or settings issue — diagnosed and fixed in a single visit in most cases.

03

TV won't turn on

Standby light blinking or completely dead? We check the power supply and mainboard.

04

Lines or spots on screen

Vertical/horizontal lines and patches traced to the T-Con, cable or panel.

05

Wi-Fi & apps not working

Smart TV stuck on logo, apps crashing or network not connecting — we restore it.

06

Remote & ports faulty

Unresponsive remote, broken HDMI/USB ports or no channel signal — all repaired.
