Who We Are

Honest TV Repair, Delivered to Your Door

doorsteptvservice.in grew out of a small neighbourhood TV service centre with one simple belief: people deserve repair work they can trust, without the hassle of carrying a heavy TV across town.

Over 15 years, our technicians have worked on everything from picture-tube sets to today's 4K OLED and QLED Smart TVs. We repair at component level wherever possible, which often saves you the cost of replacing an entire board or panel.

Every visit starts with a proper diagnosis and a clear quote. You decide — and only then do we begin.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair the TV at my home?

Yes. Most repairs are completed at your home in a single visit. If a board needs bench-level work, we explain why, take it to our workshop and return it quickly.

Is there a visiting or inspection charge?

A small inspection charge applies, and it is adjusted in the final bill if you go ahead with the repair. You'll always know the cost before we begin.

Do you give a warranty on repairs?

Yes, every repair comes with a service warranty on the parts replaced and the work done. The exact period depends on the part and will be mentioned on your bill.

Which brands do you service?

We service all major brands including Samsung, LG, Sony, Mi, OnePlus, TCL, VU, Panasonic, Philips, Hisense and many more.

How soon can a technician visit?

For most bookings we offer same-day visits. Call or WhatsApp us early in the day for the fastest slot.
